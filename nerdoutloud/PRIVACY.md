## 1. Overview
NerdOutLoud is built privacy-first. This version of the Service has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics trackers, and no sale of personal information.

## 2. What we collect
Your movie library, ratings, predictions, comments, display handle, and settings are stored locally in your own browser's storage on your device. We do not transmit this information to our own servers in this version of the Service. If community features are enabled in a hosted environment, comments and handles you post may be stored by that environment and visible to other users — post only what you are comfortable sharing publicly.

## 3. Local storage, not tracking
The Service uses your browser's local storage strictly to make the app work (saving your library and preferences between visits). We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking.

## 4. Third-party resources
To run, the Service loads fonts from Google Fonts and JavaScript libraries from public CDNs (such as unpkg.com). When your browser fetches those files, those providers receive standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type, governed by their own privacy policies. We do not control those providers.

## 5. Children
The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a community feature, contact us and we will address it.

## 6. Your choices and deletion
Because your data lives in your browser, you are in control: clearing your browser's site data for NerdOutLoud permanently deletes your library, ratings, and settings. To request removal of a comment posted in a shared community space, contact us at [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL].

## 7. Security
No method of storage is 100% secure. Data kept in your browser is protected by your device's own security; anyone with access to your device and browser profile could view it.

## 8. Changes to this policy
If we add features that change how data is handled (for example, user accounts or a community backend), we will update this policy and its effective date before those features go live.
